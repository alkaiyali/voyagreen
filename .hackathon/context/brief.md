# VoyaGreen — brief (from the team)

## 1. Product Overview
A mobile-first travel recommendation platform designed to help tourists make more environmentally responsible travel decisions. Instead of recommending destinations based only on popularity, price, or distance, VoyaGreen considers tourism pressure and environmental indicators to suggest lower-pressure alternatives.
The hackathon MVP focuses on a simple user journey: the traveler selects a destination, VoyaGreen evaluates its tourism pressure, recommends a greener alternative when appropriate, and generates a personalized itinerary based on the traveler's interests and trip duration.

## 2. Problem Statement
Tourism is often concentrated in a small number of highly popular destinations. This can contribute to overcrowding, pressure on local resources, and environmental degradation. Travelers may also have limited visibility into the environmental pressure associated with their destination choices.
VoyaGreen addresses this problem by turning sustainability information into a practical travel recommendation rather than leaving the traveler to research it independently.
